You are a Lead Digital Architect specializing in luxury hospitality with a deep understanding of user experience and high-end design principles. 

Your task is to design a high-end, multi-page digital menu that functions as a seamless web application. Here are the key requirements: 

1. Global Navigation & Language Control
- The Top Navbar: A persistent, sticky header across all pages.
- Language Switcher: Located in the top right, featuring a clean toggle for EN | FR | AR.
- Functionality: Selecting a language must instantly update the entire UI—including category titles, dish descriptions, and currency formatting—while maintaining the user's current scroll position.
- RTL Support: Ensure the layout mirrors correctly (Right-to-Left) when AR is selected, adjusting the grid and text alignment accordingly.

2. The Menu Hub (Landing Page)
- Layout: A minimalist grid showing only the Main Category Titles (e.g., Starters, Signature Mains, Wood-Fired Pizzas, Desserts).
- Visuals: Each category cell features a high-resolution, "mood-lit" background image with a subtle dark overlay for text contrast.
- Interaction: These titles are Smart Triggers. Clicking one must navigate the user to a dedicated, standalone page for that specific section.

3. The Category Sub-Pages
- Each section (e.g., "Signature Mains") exists on its own page to ensure a focused, clutter-free experience.
- Sticky Header: Includes a "← Back to Main Menu" link and the language switcher.
- Item Architecture: Food items are housed in "frosted glass" cards with a subtle blur effect and a 1px gold border.
- Typography: Bold, elegant serif for Dish Names; clean sans-serif for descriptions.
- Details: Every dish must display its Name, a Sensory Description, and a Price (e.g., $24).
- Transitions: Use fluid page transitions (e.g., a soft fade or slide) to make the movement between the Hub and Sub-pages feel instantaneous.

4. Design Aesthetic & Accessibility
- Color Palette: Charcoal (#1A1A1A) for backgrounds, Champagne Gold (#D4AF37) for accents/prices, and Cream (#F5F5F5) for primary text.
- Responsive Design: Mobile: A vertical, thumb-friendly stack. Desktop: A sophisticated multi-column masonry layout for food items.
- Accessibility: Ensure high color contrast and ARIA labels for the language
